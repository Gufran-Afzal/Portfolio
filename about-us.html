My approach

I don’t just build what looks good. I build what makes sense.

Every ecommerce business is different. That's why I start by understanding the problem before jumping into development.

  1. Understand

    I learn about your business, products, customers, and project goals.

  2. Plan

    I define the structure, features, and Shopify approach for the project.

  3. Build
