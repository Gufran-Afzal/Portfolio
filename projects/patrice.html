Overview

For Patrice Fransez, I worked on targeted improvements to the existing Shopify storefront, focusing on refining the user experience and addressing areas where small changes could make the shopping journey smoother. The work involved practical theme adjustments tailored to the store's needs.

The challenge

The goal was to improve the existing storefront without undertaking a full redesign, focusing on specific areas where usability and the overall shopping experience could be refined.

The solution

I made focused adjustments to the Shopify theme to improve the storefront experience and refine key interface elements. Rather than rebuilding existing functionality, I concentrated on practical improvements that fit the current design and business requirements.

What I delivered

  • Targeted Shopify theme adjustments
  • Storefront usability refinements
  • User experience improvements
  • Conversion-focused interface updates

Results

  • Refined the existing storefront without requiring a complete redesign
  • Addressed selected usability and shopping experience improvements
  • Delivered focused theme customizations aligned with the brand
  • Improved key interface details while retaining the existing storefront structure
