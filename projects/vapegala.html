Overview

VapeGala needed a refreshed storefront that delivered a more consistent and intuitive shopping experience. I redesigned the entire store, reworking every page to create a unified visual language, improve content hierarchy, and make product discovery and navigation feel more seamless.

The challenge

The main challenge was to redesign the complete storefront rather than focusing on individual pages. Every page needed to feel connected, remain easy to navigate, and support a consistent shopping experience across desktop and mobile devices.

The solution

I carried out a full storefront redesign, updating page layouts, visual styling, content structure, and responsive behavior throughout the store. The redesign brought greater consistency to the customer journey while creating a cleaner, more cohesive interface across the entire shopping experience.

What I delivered

  • Complete storefront redesign
  • Redesigned layouts across all pages
  • Updated product and collection experiences
  • Consistent UI and visual styling
  • Responsive storefront layouts
  • Customized Shopify theme

Results

  • Delivered a complete redesign of the entire Shopify storefront
  • Established a consistent visual identity across all website pages
  • Improved page layouts and content hierarchy throughout the store
  • Created a more cohesive product browsing and shopping experience
  • Implemented responsive layouts for desktop and mobile devices
  • Customized the Shopify theme to align with the refreshed storefront design
