Overview

Study Abroad by TRC is a custom website project I developed from scratch for an international education consultancy. The website was built to communicate the organization's services, organize important information for prospective students, and provide a clear, accessible online experience tailored to the brand.

The challenge

The website needed to present study abroad services and educational information in a structured, approachable way while establishing trust with prospective students. The challenge was to create a cohesive website experience that made information easy to navigate across different devices.

The solution

I designed and developed the website from the ground up, creating custom page layouts, front-end styling, and navigation tailored to the project's requirements. I focused on clear content hierarchy, consistent visual presentation, and responsive layouts to make the website accessible across desktop and mobile devices.

What I delivered

  • Complete website development from scratch
  • Custom page layouts and styling
  • Responsive website design
  • Service and information page structure
  • Custom navigation and content organization
  • Front-end interactions
  • Consistent visual styling across pages
  • Cross-device layout refinement

Results

  • Delivered a fully custom-built website for the consultancy
  • Created a structured online presence for study abroad services
  • Organized educational information for easier navigation
  • Established a consistent visual experience across the website
  • Provided responsive layouts for desktop and mobile users
