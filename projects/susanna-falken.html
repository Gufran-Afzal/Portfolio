Overview

Susanna Falken already had a working jewellery store, but the design no longer matched where the brand was heading. The goal was a considered redesign, not a rebuild from zero: keep what worked, rework what did not.

The challenge

Navigation and product pages had grown cluttered over time, which made browsing feel heavier than the products deserved.

The solution

I simplified the information hierarchy, refined the typography and spacing, and reworked the collection and product templates so the imagery leads and the interface gets out of the way.

What I delivered

  • Redesigned collection and product templates
  • Simplified navigation
  • Typography and spacing system
  • Mobile-first QA across devices
