Overview

SR Beauty was a complex e-commerce project that required much more than a standard Shopify theme. I built the store from scratch, developing a custom storefront around a catalogue of more than 4,000 products while accommodating the distinct needs of both business customers and individual consumers. The project combined custom theme development, tailored functionality, and a scalable structure designed to support a demanding retail operation.

The challenge

The main challenge was bringing a large product catalogue, B2B and B2C purchasing requirements, and multiple custom functionalities together in one cohesive storefront. The system needed to handle the complexity of a substantial catalogue while providing a clear, reliable, and responsive shopping experience for different customer types.

The solution

I developed a custom Shopify theme from the ground up and built the storefront architecture around the project's specific business requirements. I implemented tailored functionality for the B2B and B2C shopping experience, structured the interface to accommodate more than 4,000 products, and customized page templates and reusable components to create a consistent experience throughout the store.

What I delivered

  • Complete Shopify store development from scratch
  • Fully custom Shopify theme
  • Storefront architecture for 4,000+ products
  • B2B and B2C shopping system
  • Custom business-specific functionalities
  • Custom product and collection page templates
  • Reusable theme sections and components
  • Responsive layouts across the storefront
  • Customized navigation and product discovery
  • Maintainable, scalable theme code

Results

  • Successfully launched a fully custom Shopify storefront built from scratch
  • Structured a catalogue of 4,000+ products within a scalable store architecture
  • Unified B2B and B2C shopping experiences within one e-commerce platform
  • Delivered custom functionality tailored to the business's operational requirements
  • Established a consistent, responsive shopping experience across the storefront
  • Created a flexible custom theme designed for ongoing maintenance and future growth
